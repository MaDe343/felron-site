# FelRon IT-Legacy-Capital: San Antonio Marketing Plan

A practical, low-budget plan built around a day job. Target: 3–5 hours a week and under $100 in the first month.

---

## 1. What I do (one line)

> "I'm the trusted first call: I fix it, source it, or line it up and compare the quotes, and I teach you how it works along the way."

## 2. 30-second pitch

> "I'm Matthew Diaz, founder of FelRon IT-Legacy-Capital here in San Antonio. FelRon is named for my parents, Felicia and Ronald. My dad was a 29-year Army combat medic and paratrooper. When something causes friction, like a computer that won't cooperate, a handful of small fixes around the house, a quote that looks too high or a crypto machine you want to buy without overpaying, I'm the one call that handles it. ## 4. Channels (in order of priority)

### Free and high-impact (week 1)
1. **Google Business Profile.** Create it as a *service-area business* (hide the home address and list San Antonio plus the nearby ZIPs). Categories: "Handyman" (primary), "Computer support and services" and "Business management consultant". Add 10+ photos (you at work, tools, a clean machine setup, before and after shots of painting), your hours and the website link. Ask every happy client for a review. Reviews are the #1 local ranking factor.
2. **Nextdoor.** Create a business page and post in your neighborhood and the nearby ones. Neighbors recommending neighbors is the strongest channel for handyman and senior help.
3. **Facebook.** Set up a business page, then join and post (following each group's rules) in groups like "San Antonio Small Business", neighborhood buy/sell/trade groups, JBSA spouse groups, and local crypto and mining groups.
4. **Facebook Marketplace (Services) and Craigslist (San Antonio > services > skilled trades / computer).** Write separate listings for: handyman and painting, tech help, quote comparison, crypto machine setup, and business paperwork.

### Gig platforms (week 2)
5. **Thumbtack, TaskRabbit and Fiverr.**
   - TaskRabbit: assembly, mounting, minor repairs, painting.
   - Thumbtack: handyman, computer help.
   - Fiverr: remote services such as business setup guidance, website help and crypto ROI reviews.
   - Use them for the first reviews, then move repeat clients to direct booking.

### Offline (ongoing, nearly free)
6. **Flyers and business cards.**
   - Print 250 cards and 50 flyers (about $40–60 at Vistaprint or a local print shop).
   - Put a QR code to the website and the phone number on every piece.
   - Leave a stack at the smoke shop (ask your manager first). Ask nearby barbershops, laundromats, coffee shops, auto parts stores, senior centers and church bulletin boards.
   - Hand a card to every customer who mentions a problem.

### Short video (2–3 per week, 30–60 seconds)
7. **X and TikTok (and Instagram Reels and YouTube Shorts, which take the same clip).**
   - Show, don't sell: "How I checked if this Antminer quote was fair", "3 things to check before you call for AC service", "Hanging a TV the right way", "What a DBA is in 60 seconds".
   - End every video with "What's the friction? Link in bio."

### Referral offer
8. **"Give $10, get $10."** A referred friend gets $10 off their first paid job, and the referrer gets $10 off their next one. Print it on the back of the business card.

## 8. Legal framing (use everywhere)

- **Education and guidance on flat fees.** I teach, guide and do hands-on help. Clients make their own decisions.
- **No investment advice.** Markets content is always "what I do personally, for education". Never tell someone what to buy, and never promise returns. Add "Education only, not financial advice" to every crypto or markets post.
- **No custody.** I never hold client money, keys, seed phrases or passwords.
- **Licensed trades.** I don't advertise AC, electrical or plumbing repairs. I offer filter changes, troubleshooting checklists, plug-in machine setup, and gathering and comparing quotes from licensed pros.
- **Signed acknowledgment** (the website waiver) before paid work, plus a written recap after each session.
- **Reviews must be real.** Never post fake testimonials, and don't pay for reviews.
