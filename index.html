The FelRon story

Fel + Ron. Felicia and Ronald.

FelRon is named for my parents, Felicia and Ronald, and founded in their honor.

My father, Ronald, served 29 years as a U.S. Army combat medic and paratrooper. He fought in the Gulf War with the 2nd Armored Cavalry Regiment at the Battle of 73 Easting, served separately with the 82nd Airborne Division at Fort Bragg, and was stationed in Italy and Germany. He deployed to Iraq in 2004–05 and again for the 2007–08 surge, made more than 80 jumps, earned the Bronze Star, and retired as a Sergeant First Class from the Warrior Transition Unit at Fort Sam Houston. My mother, Felicia, earned numerous medals for her Family Readiness Group service, supporting military families through every move and deployment, while building a career in fine retail and diamond jewelry.

From them I learned composure under pressure and genuine caretaking. In our house, when family or friends hit a technical failure, a financial tangle or a logistical bottleneck, you stepped up and solved it. FelRon brings that same calm, precision and good faith to every client.

Legal

The framework I follow.

The plain rules behind every session, module, tool and job.

These are the rules I hold myself to in every conversation, online or in person. They protect you, and they keep FelRon on the right side of Texas and federal rules. This is a plain summary, not legal advice.

How Every Session Runs
  • ☑Education and options, not directives. I explain how things work and the common approaches people consider. I never tell you what to buy, sell or move.
  • ☑Signed acknowledgment first. No personal session starts without a signed Client Acknowledgment.
  • ☑Written recap after every session. It records what we covered, and that the decisions are yours.
  • ☑Speak as if recorded. Texas is a one-party-consent state, so anyone in a conversation may legally record it. I keep every conversation consistent with this framework.
  • ☑Done-with-you, never done-for-you on money. I walk you through tasks and handle the legwork, but you approve every action and place your own trades.
  • ☑Independent handyman & quotes. I line up and coordinate the independent handyman I work with for painting and easy fixes, and I gather and compare quotes from independent providers. You choose whether to hire anyone.
  • ☑Privacy and no custody. Need-to-know information only, minimal retention, and I never hold your funds, keys, passwords or logins.
  • ☑Flat, traceable fees for modules and sessions. No percentage of assets, no profit share, no commissions. See Modules & Packages.
Licensed Work, Handled the Right Way
Personalized investment advice requires registration as an investment adviser, typically including the Series 65 exam, through the Texas State Securities Board ↗. Until then, I refer you to a licensed fiduciary you can check on FINRA BrokerCheck ↗.
Paid tax return preparation requires an IRS PTIN. I organize your records and refer you to a CPA, an enrolled agent or IRS VITA ↗.
Electrical, AC and plumbing repairs are performed by licensed professionals you can verify on TDLR ↗. I gather and compare their quotes so you can choose, and I handle plug-in machine setup and configuration.
Adjusting insurance claims for others requires a Texas Department of Insurance ↗ license. I help you organize paperwork and understand your policy.
Legal advice and legal documents come from a licensed attorney, such as one found through the State Bar of Texas ↗.
Why I point you to outside resources.

The government and nonprofit links throughout this site are client referrals. They send you to the official, licensed source for anything outside education and organization.
