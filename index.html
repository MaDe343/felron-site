MODULE 07

Legal Framework I Follow

A plain checklist of the rules behind every session.

These are the rules I hold myself to in every conversation, online or in person. They protect you, and they keep FelRon on the right side of Texas and federal rules. This is a plain summary, not legal advice.

How Every Session Runs
  • ☑Education and options, not directives. I explain how things work and the common approaches people consider. I never tell you what to buy, sell or move.
  • ☑Signed acknowledgment first. No personal session starts without a signed Client Acknowledgment.
  • ☑Written recap after every session. It records what we covered, and that the decisions are yours.
  • ☑Speak as if recorded. Texas is a one-party-consent state, so anyone in a conversation may legally record it. I keep every conversation consistent with this framework.
  • ☑Privacy and no custody. Need-to-know information only, minimal retention, and I never hold your funds, keys, passwords or logins.
  • ☑Flat, traceable fees. No percentage of assets, no profit share, no commissions.
Licensed Work I Refer Out
Personalized investment advice requires registration as an investment adviser, typically including the Series 65 exam, through the Texas State Securities Board ↗. Until then, I refer you to a licensed fiduciary you can check on FINRA BrokerCheck ↗.
Paid tax return preparation requires an IRS PTIN. I organize your records and refer you to a CPA, an enrolled agent or IRS VITA ↗.
Electrical and HVAC work requires a TDLR ↗ license. I handle plug-in setup and configuration only.
Adjusting insurance claims for others requires a Texas Department of Insurance ↗ license. I help you organize paperwork and understand your policy.
Legal advice and legal documents come from a licensed attorney, such as one found through the State Bar of Texas ↗.
Why I point you to outside resources.

The government and nonprofit links throughout this site are client referrals. They send you to the official, licensed source for anything outside education and organization. That protects you, because you get the right expert. It also protects me, because it keeps FelRon clearly within what I'm allowed to do.
